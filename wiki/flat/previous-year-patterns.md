## Summary

The FLAT PYQs emphasize automata construction, regular expressions, closure properties, grammar classification, PDA/CFL facts, CNF, decidability, recursive and recursively enumerable languages, and DFA state counting.

## High-Yield Claims

> **[EXAM_TIP]** Regular languages are closed under union, concatenation, Kleene star, complement, and intersection.

> **[EXAM_TIP]** CFLs are closed under union, concatenation, and Kleene star, but not under arbitrary intersection or complement.

> **[EXAM_TIP]** If both `L` and complement of `L` are recursively enumerable, then `L` is recursive.

## Recurring PYQ Areas

- Regular languages: regex design, closure, pumping lemma use, finite-language regularity.
- Automata: DFA/NFA conversion, Moore-machine output, DFA state counts, epsilon-closures.
- CFG and PDA: odd palindromes, PDA stack memory, CNF, unit productions, useless productions.
- Chomsky hierarchy: regular, CFL, CSL, unrestricted, Type 0 grammar classification.
- Decidability: recursive languages, R.E. languages, halting problem, language complement.
