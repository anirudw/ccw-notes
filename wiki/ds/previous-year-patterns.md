## Summary

The DS PYQs focus on fundamental operations and cost analysis: BST insertion/search, tree traversal, recursion stacks, queues, hashing, heap sort, insertion sort, quicksort, expression conversion, and linked-list traversal.

## High-Yield Claims

> **[EXAM_TIP]** For sorting questions, memorize best, average, and worst-case complexity separately. Bubble sort with an early-exit flag has best-case `O(n)`, quicksort has worst-case `O(n^2)`, and heap sort is `O(n log n)` in the worst case.

> **[EXAM_TIP]** Recursion uses the call stack; BFS uses a queue; expression conversion and evaluation often use a stack.

> **[CONNECTION]** Hashing in DS supports [DBMS Indexing and Buffering](../dbms/indexing-and-buffering.md), especially hash-based file organization and indexing.

## Recurring PYQ Areas

- Binary search trees: insertion height, inorder traversal, search path validity, predecessor properties.
- Trees and heaps: postorder traversal, full `k`-ary tree leaf count, heap-sort states.
- Linear structures: stack overflow/underflow, queue front/rear states, linked-list traversal loops.
- Sorting and searching: quicksort partition, insertion-sort comparisons, bubble-sort swaps, sequential-search average comparisons.
- Hashing: collisions and separate chaining.
- Expressions: infix, prefix, postfix conversion and postfix evaluation.
